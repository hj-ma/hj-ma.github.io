Interest

Guzheng

I have practiced Guzheng for more than 12 years. Over time, it changed from a required childhood activity into a long-term form of discipline and personal expression.

Why it matters to me

  • Long-term practice taught patience and consistency.
  • Music gave me a stronger appreciation for craft and repetition.
  • It remains one of the clearest examples in my life of how persistence turns into genuine interest.
