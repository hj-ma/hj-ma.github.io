Guzheng & Football

Guzheng and football give me two very different ways to practice discipline. Guzheng taught me patience, rhythm, and respect for slow improvement; football trains quick decisions, constant movement, and trust in teammates under pressure.

Guzheng

  • Practiced for more than 12 years, growing from a childhood activity into a long-term form of personal expression.
  • Learned consistency through repetition, careful listening, and attention to small details.
  • Built patience and control through traditional music practice.

Football

  • Play on my college women's football team, mainly as a winger or forward.
  • Practice reading space, coordinating runs, and reacting to fast-changing situations.
  • Learn teamwork through training, match preparation, and learning from each game.
