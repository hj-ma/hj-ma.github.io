Music & Team Sport

Music and football matter to me as shared and continuing practices rather than as claims of expertise. I enjoy learning how an instrument feels and sounds, and I value football most as a way to train, move, and spend time with a team.

Music

  • Guzheng is my longest-running musical practice.
  • After entering university, I also took a few introductory piano and guitar lessons and continued exploring them casually.
  • I value the curiosity and attention involved in learning sound more than collecting proficiency labels.

Football

  • I train with my college football team and have started in matches.
  • I do not follow football closely or present myself as a highly skilled player.
  • What keeps me involved is the collective feeling of practicing, moving, and spending time with teammates.
