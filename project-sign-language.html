Project

Real-Time Sign Language Translation iOS App

This research project focuses on turning continuous sign language into readable text in real time through an iOS application. My work spans both research and implementation: reading literature, translating ideas into product decisions, and building the application workflow itself.

What I worked on

  • Reviewed more than 20 papers to map the current state of sign language recognition and translation systems.
  • Led a team of 3 undergraduate students while coordinating with graduate collaborators and the PI.
  • Built the iOS app structure in Swift and Xcode, including a UI for uploading new vocabulary.
  • Participated in refining the continuous translation algorithm for online real-time use.
