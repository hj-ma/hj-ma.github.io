A Dream of Confucius

A Dream of Confucius is an AI-assisted short film I participated in creating. The project combines script development, visual concept design, and AI-generated imagery to explore how emerging tools can support cinematic storytelling.

My contribution

  • Participated in shaping the story, scene structure, and production workflow.
  • Helped connect narrative ideas with visual direction and AI-generation prompts.
  • Practiced iterative review across script, image, motion, and editing decisions.

What I learned

  • AI video production still depends on strong human judgment in rhythm, tone, and continuity.
  • Prompting works best when paired with clear storyboards and precise visual references.
  • Collaborative creative work benefits from both technical experimentation and careful critique.
